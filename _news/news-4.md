The **2025 International Conference on Migrasome** opened on December 23, 2025 in Nanping, Fujian — the hometown of migrasome discoverer Prof. Li Yu. The four-day meeting was hosted by the National Key Laboratory of Membrane Biology, with 30+ invited speakers from home and abroad.
