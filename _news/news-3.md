The **SMART Symposium: the First International Conference on Migrasome**, co-hosted by the Shenzhen Medical Academy of Research and Translation (SMART) and Shenzhen Bay Laboratory (SZBL), was successfully held at the Gaoke Innovation Center. Chaired by Prof. Li Yu (Tsinghua University) and Prof. Lin Deng (SZBL), the meeting brought together 200+ researchers to discuss migrasome biogenesis, function, clinical applications and research techniques.
